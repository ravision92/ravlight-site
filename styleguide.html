Writing

Rules for every page

Write for someone holding a cable. Short, concrete, in their words.

No long dashes

Split the sentence or use a comma.

Veyron — a pixel bar — runs on Art-Net.
Veyron is a pixel bar. It runs on Art-Net.

Lede under 25 words

The first line says what it is and who it is for.

Two rows of RGB pixels and six white segments in one metre.

Numbers over adjectives

A figure beats a claim.

An ultra-bright, powerful bar.
200 W, 40 pixels, 12,000 lm of white.

One idea per sentence

If a sentence needs a semicolon, it is two sentences.

Two sentences per intro

Section intros stop at two sentences. Details go in lists and tables.

No framing tricks

Skip "not X, but Y", "here's the thing", and colons that build up a reveal.

It isn't a gimmick: it's a tool.
Macros keep the colour you patched.

Name what people see

Use the words on the label and in the interface, not the code.

Configure the ESP-NOW transport.
Turn on wireless discovery.

Every claim is checkable

No "industry-leading", no "seamless". If we cannot measure it, we do not say it.

Open firmware and hardware for stage lighting.
